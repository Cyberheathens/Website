# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Current and prospective IISER Bhopal students, club members, alumni, collaborators, and recruiters. Visitors should be able to understand what Cyberheathens builds, discover its people and work, and find a clear path into the community.

## Product Purpose

Present Cyberheathens as IISER Bhopal's active coding club and give visitors an approachable way to explore its projects, events, learning resources, team, and alumni. Success means a visitor can quickly understand the club, discover relevant work, and know how to participate.

## Brand Personality

Experimental, precise, welcoming. The experience should feel technically credible and tactile without becoming an insider-only developer joke. Interaction should reward curiosity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, decorative terminal motifs with no useful behavior, excessive glass cards, synthetic testimonials, and cyberpunk effects that reduce readability. Do not present placeholder alumni content as a real endorsement.

## Design Principles

1. Make technical culture tangible through interactions that perform a real task.
2. Let visitors explore at their own level, from obvious navigation to discoverable terminal commands.
3. Show the club through specific projects, people, events, and outcomes rather than generic claims.
4. Treat alumni voices as verified community history, never invented marketing copy.
5. Keep ambitious motion subordinate to readability, performance, and user control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operability. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide a visible sound toggle, never make audio essential to understanding the interface, and preserve usable alternatives to terminal commands through standard page navigation.
